Frequently Asked Questions

Clear Answers for Your Wellness Journey.

Do I have to follow restrictive starvation or give up family meals?

No. Coach Aruna's philosophy strictly rejects starvation diets. You will learn proper portion awareness, nutrient timing, and balanced home-cooked meals that nourish your body sustainably without disconnecting you from family dining.

Is online coaching available if I live outside Una, Gujarat?

Yes, absolutely. Garima Wellness Center offers dedicated Online Health Coaching. Coach Aruna guides clients remotely via scheduled phone calls, WhatsApp check-ins, and digital progress tracking.

How is weight management personalized for each individual?

Every body type, metabolic rate, work routine, and health history is unique. We evaluate your daily activity, water intake, sleep rhythm, and specific goals—whether targeting stubborn visceral belly fat or relieving joint fatigue.

Do you also help with healthy weight gain or post-recovery strengthening?

Yes. Wellness is not just about losing weight; it is equally about rebuilding undernourished bodies. Our verified client transformations include rebuilding from post-hospital weakness (45 kg) to a healthy, energized 55 kg.
